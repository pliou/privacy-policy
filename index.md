# Privacy Policy for PPL Extensions (Scored Search & Order Metrics)

**Last updated:** 15.08.2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Pawel Pliousnin Digital Solutions ("we", "our", or "us") handles information in relation to the following extensions:

- Scored Search for Magento / Adobe Commerce  
- Order Metrics for Magento / Adobe Commerce  

## General Statement

Our extensions are designed to operate within the Magento / Adobe Commerce environment and do not independently collect or store personal data outside of that system.

Magento or Adobe Commerce itself may process personal data as part of its normal operation. This is outside the scope of our extensions.

## Information We Collect

- We do **not** directly collect or store personal data.  
- Our extensions do not include tracking, analytics, or profiling functionality.  
- If third-party services are integrated by the merchant, only the minimum data required for functionality may be transmitted.

## How We Use Information

Any data processed by our extensions is used exclusively for:

- Providing core functionality  
- Improving system performance and search accuracy (Scored Search)  
- Processing and displaying order-related metrics (Order Metrics)  

We do **not**:

- Sell personal data  
- Share data for marketing purposes  
- Use data for profiling or advertising  

## Data Storage

- Our extensions do **not** store personal data.  
- All data processing occurs within the Magento / Adobe Commerce system and is controlled by the merchant.
- Our extensions do not create independent databases, external storage, or remote data persistence.

## Hosting & Server Logs

While our extensions do not collect personal data, the hosting environment (e.g., web server, Magento system) may automatically log technical data such as:

- IP addresses  
- Request logs  
- System events  

This is standard server behavior and not controlled by our extensions.

## Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data processed within the scope of our extensions.

However, overall system security depends on the Magento / Adobe Commerce installation and hosting environment.

## Third-Party Services

If the merchant integrates third-party services, those services may process data according to their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for third-party data handling.
